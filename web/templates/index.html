      How to use

      1

      Pick city + locality

      Click the City and Locality fields to see curated suggestions, or just type any neighbourhood — we'll search live.

      2

      Set your filters

      BHK, monthly rent range, furnishing. These are hard filters — listings outside the price range or with the wrong BHK are dropped, not just down-ranked.

      3

      Hit Search

      All sources run in parallel. You'll see results stream in, then a final deduped + ranked list with a match score. Click any card to open the original listing.

      Tip: a wider price range usually means more results to dedupe across sources. If you get "no listings matched", the most common fix is widening the rent range or setting BHK / Furnishing to Any. Listings whose price couldn't be parsed are kept and ranked neutrally.
